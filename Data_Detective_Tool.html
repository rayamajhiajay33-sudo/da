Exact duplicate records

Exact duplicates match across every column. Choose which later copies to mark for exclusion from the cleaned export.

Potential duplicates by selected columns

Potential duplicates only share the selected column values. They are not automatically considered duplicates; review each group before excluding any record.

Conflicting identifiers

A conflict means the same nonblank ID is associated with different information. Conflicts are listed separately and are never automatically merged or removed.

Duplicate-handling decisions

Select records from the exact-duplicate and potential-duplicate groups above, then explicitly mark them for exclusion. Conflicting-ID records remain unless you manually resolve their values.

User-defined validation rules

Add checks such as required values, minimum/maximum numbers, allowed values or regular-expression patterns. These checks report issues; they do not change data.
